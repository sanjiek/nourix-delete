Delete Your Nourix Account

You can delete your account directly from the app at any time.

How to delete in the app

  1. 1
    Open Nourix on your Android device
  2. 2
    Tap the Profile tab at the bottom of the screen
  3. 3
    Scroll down and tap Settings
  4. 4
    Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account
  5. 5
    Confirm the deletion. Your account and all data will be permanently removed
⚠️ This action is irreversible. All your calorie logs, goals, and personal data will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
